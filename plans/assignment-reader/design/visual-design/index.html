State model

processing, review, grading
Processing
Uploading→Queued→Transcribing→Completed

Runs per page. Error stays recoverable and does not become a grading outcome.

Review
Needs review→Ready to grade

Teacher verifies every page against its source. Materials end as verified context.

Grading
Not graded→Graded

Student submissions only. Score and comments remain optional and teacher-owned.
